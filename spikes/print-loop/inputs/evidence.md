# Evidence (input to the agent)

Paraphrased from public reviews found by web search on 2026-11-06. These are summaries,
not raw Amazon reviews; the real loop replaces this file with exported review text
(see the experiment guide, step A1).

## Demand
- Apple AirTag (2nd gen): #1 in Item Finders on Amazon US, about 101,931 units in the
  30 days before 2026-10-12 (AMZScout estimate,
  https://amzscout.net/blog/best-selling-products-on-amazon/).

## airtag-no-hole
- "No keyring hole" is the most repeated complaint: buyers need a separate holder or case
  (ShopSavvy review summaries; Expert Reviews,
  https://www.expertreviews.co.uk/technology/accessories/apple-airtag-2nd-gen-review).
- Same body as gen 1, so gen 1 holders fit gen 2 (Expert Reviews: "same design as before").

## Positives to keep
- Louder speaker and longer range (Cult of Mac,
  https://www.cultofmac.com/reviews/airtag-2-review-better-than-the-original),
  so a holder must not muffle the speaker.

## Risks
- Holder market is crowded. This product tests the loop, not a market gap.
- Do not use "Apple" or "AirTag" as a brand name; "for AirTag" wording needs an IP check
  (experiment guide, step H6).
